Brand references · profile marks

Six marks for
HEIRLOM

Drawn against the game’s own palette, sized for the one place they have to work: a circular avatar in a timeline, at twenty‑four pixels.

6 concepts 512×512 SVG, full bleed palette from globals.css in docs/brand/

Every mark was rendered and checked at 24 px before it was kept — that is the size an avatar actually appears at beside a post, and it is the only size that decides anything. Two of the first six failed that test and were redrawn. None of them contains text: at avatar size a wordmark is illegible, and the handle already does that job an inch to the right.

What I’d ship

Two answers, depending on whether you are optimising for the first glance or for the next five years.

02 · Helix sprout

the avatar

A stranger decodes it without context, and it stops the account reading as a generic farm game — which is the real positioning problem, since the genetics are the product.

03 · Punnett

the long game

The sharper brand. Unmistakable at any size, carries the recessive‑morph idea, and does not compete with a thousand other leaf‑in‑a‑circle avatars. Reads least like a game — that is the trade.

06 · Herbarium tag

the header

Too detailed for the circle, exactly right at banner width — and it is already the object the game prints on every pressed specimen.
